Projects

Design optimization of vortex cups using FMQA and CFD

BSc thesis · 2025–2027 · co-supervised with Kobayashi Wataru

A factorization machine learns a QUBO surrogate from OpenFOAM simulations; quantum annealing (Fixstars Amplify) proposes the next design. The loop improved suction force by 5×. Now extending to more design parameters to raise optimization efficiency.

IoT flood-sensor placement via QUBO

Collaboration with Chiang Mai University · upcoming

Minimize the number of flood sensors while covering a wide area, as the backbone of a citizen smartphone alert system.
